prashna

Live Q&A and quizzes for any session with an audience

For trainers, teachers, workshop and meetup hosts

The problem

Many people will not ask a question out loud.

A presenter cannot easily see who is following.

What Prashna gives
  • Anonymous questions with upvotes
  • Short quizzes
  • One shared screen
